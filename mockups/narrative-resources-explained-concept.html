Saint Gobain AS2 PKG3 — baseline

13) Resource Loading

13.1 Manpower

(chart: man-hours per month, January 2025 – August 2025, peak May 2025 — unchanged)

How these numbers are calculated 1. Each Labour resource assignment carries its budgeted man-hours in P6.
2. Those man-hours are spread evenly over the activity's working days on its own calendar (P6 Resource Usage), and added up per month → man-hours per month (the bars).
3. The number of workers on site in a month = man-hours in the month ÷ (working hours per day × working days in the month) on the labour calendar.
Peak, May 2025: 33,727 man-hours ÷ (11 h/day × 25 working days on "6 Days Per Week (AS3)") = ≈ 123 workers
Total for the project: 124,142 man-hours (the per-resource totals are listed below — Steel Fixer 43,432 · Carpenter 35,841 · …).

A second line under the chart gives the ≈ workers for every month, so each bar shows both: May-25 · 33,727 man-hours · ≈ 123 workers.

13.2 Equipment

(chart: number of machines on site per month — unchanged)

How these numbers are calculated In this schedule the equipment is loaded in hours (equipment-hours), so the hours are turned into machines:
1. Machines on an activity = its budgeted equipment-hours ÷ the activity's working hours.
2. Each month counts those machines over the working days they are loaded ÷ the working days of the month.
Example — Water Truck: 352 equipment-hours on an activity of 176 working hours (16 days × 11 h) = 2 trucks for those 16 days
Totals per resource are in equipment-hours (Dumper Truck 4,257 · Loader 4,257 · …) with the peak number on site.

14) Material Resources

No material quantities to chart in this schedule. The material resources in this P6 file have no unit of measure assigned, so their budgeted units cannot be read as quantities (m³, ton, m², …). The only one used — “EX-resource” — carries the cost loading: 243,805,397 across 442 assignments, which is the contract value shown in section 6, not a material quantity.

To show material quantities here: in P6, give each material resource a unit of measure (Admin ▸ Admin Categories ▸ Units of Measure, then the resource's Unit of Measure) and load the quantities on the activities; re-export and run the report again.
